Verbatim opening:
> "Edge Attribution Patching (EAP) is a gradient-based method for mechanistic interpretability that uses a first-order Taylor approximation to assess edge importance in neural networks."

Synthesis: The topic page collates the literature defining and extending Edge Attribution Patching as an efficient gradient-based surrogate for activation patching at the edge level of a model's computational graph. It introduces EAP as a scalable circuit-discovery primitive — assigning importance scores to edges via a single forward and backward pass — and tracks subsequent work that probes its faithfulness, robustness, and saturation behaviour, including extensions that mitigate gradient-saturation artefacts. The page is intended as a research-orientation entry point linking the foundational paper to follow-up developments.
